# Differential Geometry

This category covers all the essentials of an introductory course in differential geometry, benchmarked against the chapter structure of the introductory portions of Chen Weihuan's *Differential Geometry* and Do Carmo's *Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces*, running from the classical theory of curves and surfaces all the way to a first look at manifolds and Riemannian geometry.
