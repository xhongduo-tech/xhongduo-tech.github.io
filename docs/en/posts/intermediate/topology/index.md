# Topology

Topology studies the properties of spaces that are preserved under continuous deformation; it is the common language of analysis, geometry, and algebra. This section follows the chapter structure of the introductory portions of 尤承业《基础拓扑学讲义》 and Munkres' *Topology*, covering all the core content of point-set topology and the basics of algebraic topology.
