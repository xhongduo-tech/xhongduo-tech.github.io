# Abstract Algebra

A complete walkthrough of modern algebra: starting from algebraic structures and homomorphisms/isomorphisms, this series systematically covers the three great algebraic structures — groups, rings, and fields — along with an introduction to Galois theory. It follows the chapter layout of Yang Zixu's *Modern Algebra* and Qiu Weisheng's *Foundations of Abstract Algebra*, with each section mapping to a blog post.
