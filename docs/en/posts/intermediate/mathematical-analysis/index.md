# Mathematical Analysis

Mathematical analysis is calculus made rigorous. Built on the ε-δ language and the completeness of the real numbers, it rebuilds the entire proof system of limits, continuity, differentiation, integration, and series. This category follows the chapter structure of East China Normal University's *Mathematical Analysis*, complementing the engineering-oriented *Advanced Mathematics* (Tongji edition): the latter emphasizes computation, while this one emphasizes proof.
