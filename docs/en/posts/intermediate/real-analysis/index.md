# Real Analysis & Measure Theory

Real analysis deepens mathematical analysis: using set theory and measure theory as tools, it rebuilds the theory of integration (the Lebesgue integral) and studies the measurability, integrability, and differentiation structure of functions. This section follows the chapter structure of Zhou Minqiang's *Theory of Functions of Real Variables* and I. P. Natanson's *Theory of Functions of a Real Variable*, starting from sets and cardinal numbers, moving through measure, measurable functions, and the Lebesgue integral, and ending with differentiation, indefinite integrals, and L^p spaces.
