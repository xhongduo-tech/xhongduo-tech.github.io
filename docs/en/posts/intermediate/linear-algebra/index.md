# Linear Algebra

The tool for understanding high-dimensional spaces, the geometric language of deep learning. This category aligns with every chapter of Tongji University's *Linear Algebra*, supplemented with Gilbert Strang's *Introduction to Linear Algebra* for topics such as the four fundamental subspaces, least squares, the SVD, and data science applications.
