# My Technical Research

From limits to large language models — first lay the foundations, then understand the machines, and finally make machines do more.

This is my technical research space, organized along **four pillars**: Mathematics & Physics → Computer Science → AI & Large Models → Engineering. Each topic follows authoritative textbooks, written chapter by chapter. All of human knowledge beyond technology (philosophy, humanities, social sciences, medicine, agriculture) lives in the [Human Knowledge Tree](/en/knowledge-tree).

> English pages are available for the core topics listed below; the complete set of technical topics is maintained in the [Chinese blog](/posts/). Non-technical knowledge is preserved as knowledge-tree entries, not blog posts.
