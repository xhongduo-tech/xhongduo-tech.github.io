# Foundations of Physics

Foundations of Physics covers the full scope of high-school physics, aligned with the People's Education Press (PEP) high-school physics textbooks (Required Volumes 1–3 and Elective Required Volumes 1–3), working from motion and interactions all the way to the atomic nucleus. By the standard of "finishing a discipline = writing all the posts corresponding to the classic textbooks of that discipline," every chapter and topic is listed below.
