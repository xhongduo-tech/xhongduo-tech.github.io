# Logic

The standard for completing a discipline is writing all the blog posts that correspond to that discipline's classic textbook. This post covers the entirety of a university general-education logic course: from an introduction to logic, propositional logic and predicate logic, to traditional term logic, inductive logic, non-classical logic, paradoxes, fallacies and argumentation theory, and finally to the applications of logic in mathematics and computer science.
