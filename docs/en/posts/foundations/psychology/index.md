# Psychology

Finishing General Psychology means finishing this category. Mirroring the chapter structure of Peng Danling's *General Psychology*, it covers the object of study in psychology, its physiological basis, basic mental processes and individual psychology, and extends into developmental, social, abnormal and positive psychology. The cognitive mechanisms of perception, attention, memory and language belong to [Cognitive Science](../cognitive-science/), so this category does not repeat them.
