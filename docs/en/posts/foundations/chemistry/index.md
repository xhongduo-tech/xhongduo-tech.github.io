# Chemistry

Chemistry is the science that studies the composition, structure, properties, and patterns of change of matter at the atomic and molecular level. This category is aligned with the People's Education Press (PEP) high school chemistry curriculum (two required volumes plus three selective-compulsory volumes); each topic is written up as soon as it is studied.
