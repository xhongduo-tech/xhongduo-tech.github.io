# History & Philosophy of Science

From Thales gazing at the stars to the foundations of quantum mechanics, the history of science records every leap in how humanity has tried to understand nature; the philosophy of science asks how science is possible, how it progresses, and whether it approaches truth. This category offers a general-education panorama from ancient natural philosophy to the contemporary Science Wars, its chapters tracking classic works such as Dampier's *A History of Science*, Cohen's *The Birth of a New Physics* and *Revolution in Science*, Kuhn's *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions*, and Rosenberg's *Philosophy of Science*.
