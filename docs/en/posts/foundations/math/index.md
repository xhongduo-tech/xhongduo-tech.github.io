# Foundations of Mathematics

Guided by the People's Education Press (A-version) high-school mathematics curriculum (Compulsory Book 1, Compulsory Book 2, and Selective Compulsory Books 1–3), this systematically rebuilds the complete mathematical foundation, from sets and functions through derivatives, probability, and statistics. Work through these chapters and you will have covered the entirety of high-school mathematics.
