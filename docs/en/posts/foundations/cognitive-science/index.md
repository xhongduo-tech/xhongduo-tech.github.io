# Cognitive Science

This post is the blog writing plan for the discipline of Cognitive Science, covering the full scope of an introduction to the field. It aligns with classic textbooks such as *Cognitive Psychology* and introductory cognitive science course curricula, ranging from the history and paradigms of cognitive science, to the brain and neural foundations, sensation and perception, attention, memory, language, thought, and decision-making, and on to consciousness, development, social cognition, and artificial intelligence.
