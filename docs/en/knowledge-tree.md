## About this tree

All human knowledge is divided into **13 knowledge trees**: Philosophy, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Life Sciences, Earth & Space Sciences, Computer & AI, Engineering, Medicine & Health, Agriculture, Social Sciences, Humanities & Arts, and Cross-disciplinary Frontiers. Each grows level by level: **Foundations → Core → Advanced → Specialized → Frontier**.

- **Blog posts (technical topics)**: my technical research — click through to the corresponding post (tagged “Blog”).
- **Knowledge entries (non-technical topics)**: philosophy, humanities, social sciences, medicine, agriculture and other domains, fully retained as knowledge-tree structure — click through to their topic pages.
- **Reference**: cross-tree overlaps shown as references rather than duplicated topics.

This tree is the **complete interface** of all human knowledge: technical posts hang on their nodes, and non-technical domains are fully presented here too. Use the “My Technical Research / All Human Knowledge” toggle to view only my technical posts.
