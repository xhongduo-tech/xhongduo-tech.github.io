## About this tree

All human knowledge is divided into **13 knowledge trees**: Philosophy, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Life Sciences, Earth & Space Sciences, Computer & AI, Engineering, Medicine & Health, Agriculture, Social Sciences, Humanities & Arts, and Cross-disciplinary Frontiers. Each grows level by level: **Foundations → Core → Advanced → Specialized → Frontier**.

- **Existing**: disciplines with a built directory you can click into.
- **To-build**: disciplines currently missing and planned for writing (marked with an orange “To-build” tag).
- **Reference**: cross-tree overlaps shown as references rather than duplicated topics.

This site’s posts present only **technology-related content** (Computer / AI / Engineering / Math & Physics foundations); philosophy, humanities, social sciences, medicine, agriculture and other non-technical domains are retained as knowledge-tree structure to complete the full picture of human knowledge, with their topics marked “To-build”.
