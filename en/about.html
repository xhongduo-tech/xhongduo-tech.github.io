About

Xu Hongduo ​

I work on LLM deployment and model management platforms. Questions about latency, resources and reliability led me to trace how models generate, how training changes outputs, and how experiments locate the source of a change.

Published articles →GitHub ↗

Areas of interest ​

  • Inference algorithms and serving

    Tracing decoding, KV caches and request scheduling from the state of a single generation.

  • Training and post-training

    Following label positions, parameter updates and behavioral changes on held-out examples.

  • Model and systems evaluation

    Comparing example-level differences and investigating improvements, regressions and evaluation conditions.

About this site ​

This blog addresses specific LLM questions. Articles retain derivations, relevant source references, reproducible examples and conclusion boundaries.

The detailed learning map and annual tasks remain private. Projects become public only after real records exist. Measurements identify model versions, inputs, configurations and environments; paper results are never presented as personal measurements.
